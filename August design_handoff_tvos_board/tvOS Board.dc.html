01 · Verdict

One line at 84px answers the question before the grid does. The red bar left of it is the status field, so the headline reads at any distance even before the words resolve.

02 · The grid, kept

Five across in your NSB order — Beachway, Crawford, Flagler, 3rd, 27th. Open tiles go quiet; limited and closed become solid fields. The tile you need is the loud one.

03 · Sixteen phases

Nine on the rising track, seven falling, night and noon shared. Adds the three twilights at each end — the hours the old seven-anchor model flattened into one “night.”

04 · States

Stale drops a chip into the header and mutes every tile. Cam offline says which cam, when the last frame was, and what to press. Activity stays behind Menu.

The three stat tiles are focusable — click one for the full tide curve, both water stations and the forecast, or the wind rose and its six periods. Click anywhere to close.
