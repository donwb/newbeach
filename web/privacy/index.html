Privacy policy

Volusia Beach Info collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen, in plain language.

The apps — iPhone, iPad, Apple TV

The apps download public beach data and show it to you. That's the whole exchange. Specifically:

  • No account, no sign-in, no user profile. There is nothing to log into.
  • No analytics or telemetry. There is no third-party SDK of any kind in the apps.
  • No advertising, and no data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No access to your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or health data. The apps never ask, because they never use them.
  • Nothing is uploaded from your device. Every network request the apps make is a read of public data.
  • Ramps you favorite and the settings you choose stay on your device.

In App Store terms: Data Not Collected, and the apps do not track you.

The website

The website at beach.donwb.com keeps a basic server log of page visits so I can see whether anyone is using it. Each entry records the page path, the requesting IP address, the browser's user-agent string, and the referring page. Nothing is tied to a name or an account, because there aren't any, and the log is not shared with anyone or used for advertising.

The site loads one third-party script, the open-source hls.js video player, from the jsDelivr CDN. Fonts are served from this site, not from a font network. There are no advertising or analytics scripts.

The beach cameras

Watching a live beach camera streams video from cams.donwb.com, a server I run. As with any video stream, that server sees the request and your IP address in its ordinary access log. The cameras point at public beaches; they are wide coastal views, not anything I control, aim, or record for this project.

Where the data comes from

Ramp status comes from Volusia County's public GIS feed. Tides and water temperature come from NOAA. Weather comes from the National Weather Service. These are fetched by my server, not by your device, so those agencies never see you.

Children

The apps are rated 4+ and collect no personal information from anyone, including children.
