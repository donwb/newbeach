Large · ten ramps go two across
How it scales

The five-ramp city is the tuned case. Above five, only two things change: the strip divides into as many squares as there are ramps, and the large widget's list goes two across.

Small and medium never list ramps at all, so they are the same at any count — the verdict headline names the exceptions when there are one or two, and states the count when there are more.

Ten is the ceiling that still reads. Beyond that the large widget would need to drop to a count-only summary.

Needs from the API: a short display name per ramp. International Speedway Blvd does not fit a 162pt cell.
